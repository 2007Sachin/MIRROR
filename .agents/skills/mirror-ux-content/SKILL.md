# Mirror UX + Content

Use this skill for landing-page strategy, content hierarchy, conversion flow, research synthesis, messaging, CTA design, and trust communication. Read `mirror-product`, `mirror-frontend`, and current product capabilities before writing copy.

## Positioning rule

Mirror is not generic interview practice. It helps candidates understand whether their resume claims and interview explanations hold up against role requirements and evidence. Messaging should foreground the mechanism: resume + target role + adaptive interview + evidence-backed diagnostic.

## Research-backed content workflow

1. Start from current credible research, not generic marketing claims.
2. Prefer primary or high-authority sources such as employer research, recruiting research, academic interview-validation literature, and labor-market reports.
3. Convert research into a product problem statement only when Mirror actually addresses that problem.
4. Never exaggerate causality or invent user outcomes.
5. Keep research statistics in supporting sections; the hero should remain simple.
6. Cite sources in implementation docs or research notes when claims are numerical or externally sourced.

## Story architecture

Prefer this narrative sequence:
- What is changing in hiring/interviews?
- Why resumes and polished answers are weak evidence by themselves.
- What Mirror does differently.
- Show the mechanism in product UI.
- Show what the user receives.
- Explain trust, uncertainty, and limitations.
- Ask for the next action.

## Copy principles

- Short, concrete, precise sentences.
- Prefer `evidence`, `ownership`, `scope`, `impact`, `consistency`, `role fit`, `uncertainty`, and `diagnostic` over broad AI language.
- Avoid hype such as `ace`, `crush`, `revolutionary`, `game-changing`, `guaranteed`, `perfect score`, or `AI-powered` as the main proposition.
- Do not imply a human interviewer unless one exists.
- Do not imply lie detection, fraud detection, or hiring decisions.
- Do not claim certifications, partnerships, outcomes, or privacy guarantees that are not implemented and verified.
- Treat `not enough signal` as a valid outcome, not a failure.

## Conversion UX

A visitor should understand within five seconds:
- What Mirror is.
- What inputs it uses.
- What makes it different from a generic mock interview.
- What they receive.
- What action to take.

Use one clear primary CTA. Secondary CTAs should reduce uncertainty, not compete with conversion.

## Trust

Surface evidence traceability, no live performance scoring, explicit uncertainty, candidate-safe reporting, and diagnostic-not-hiring-authority framing. Do not bury trust in the footer.

## Completion gate

Before approving content, verify every visible capability statement against the repo. Remove duplicate sections and repeated claims. Ask whether each section earns its place by adding proof, mechanism, differentiation, trust, or conversion value.
