---
name: mirror-product
description: Product context and candidate-safety rules for Mirror.
---

Mirror diagnoses whether candidates can defend resume and role claims in an interview. Use evidence-first reasoning; keep claims, evidence, confidence, ownership, scope, impact, chronology, and uncertainty distinct. A readiness diagnosis is not a leaderboard score or placement prediction. The interview UI remains calm and focused on audio, phase, timer, and controls, never live evaluation or hidden reasoning. Check `docs/architecture/IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md` before assuming a capability exists.
